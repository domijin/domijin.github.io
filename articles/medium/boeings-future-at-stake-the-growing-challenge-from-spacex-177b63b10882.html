Chapter IV · Institutions, Incentives and Possible Futures

Boeing’s Future at Stake: The Growing Challenge from SpaceX

By Domi Jin (Dongming Jin) · May 2024

The aviation industry has long been dominated by giants like Boeing, but recent developments signal a potential shift in power. With SpaceX aggressively pushing the boundaries of aerospace technology, Boeing’s leadership in the commercial aviation space could be in jeopardy.

#### Composite Material Exploration

SpaceX has been investing heavily in the research and development of composite materials. These advanced materials, made from combining multiple elements, offer lightweight structures with high strength and durability. As a result, they can significantly reduce aircraft weight and fuel consumption, leading to increased efficiency and cost savings.

#### Innovative Manufacturing Techniques

SpaceX is not only exploring advanced materials, but also experimenting with new manufacturing techniques. They are working on methods to create large structures without relying on nuts, bolts, or welding, thus reducing the complexity and potential for failure in the assembly process. This innovative approach could revolutionize the way aircraft are built and maintained, posing a significant threat to traditional manufacturers like Boeing.

#### Business Model

Boeing’s traditional model of selling aircraft directly to airlines has shown signs of weakness in the face of macroeconomic setbacks and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. The high costs associated with purchasing aircraft make it difficult for many airlines to afford them, limiting Boeing’s potential for growth and operational stability. Conversely, SpaceX offers transportation as a service, which provides the company with more control and a shorter supply line. This approach not only reduces the cost of travel in the long run, but also makes SpaceX less dependent on external factors, both financially and in terms of management. By shifting to a service-based model, SpaceX is better positioned to adapt to market changes and maintain a competitive edge over traditional manufacturers like Boeing.

#### SpaceX’s Success in the Space Industry

SpaceX’s accomplishments in the space industry, such as its Falcon Heavy and Starlink projects, have already begun to challenge Boeing’s position in this sector. If SpaceX continues its rapid pace of innovation, the company could potentially disrupt the commercial airline space with its Starship project, further eroding Boeing’s market share.

#### Cultural Concerns

Large corporations like Boeing may struggle to maintain motivation and employee satisfaction, especially when compared to the passion and enthusiasm driving the workforce at SpaceX. A company culture that lacks momentum could prove detrimental to innovation and competitiveness, making it difficult to keep up with the fast-paced advancements of rivals like SpaceX.

In conclusion, Boeing’s future success and position in the market will depend on its ability to adapt and embrace change. The company’s board leadership team must be prepared to challenge the status quo, fostering a culture of innovation that can restore Boeing to its former glory. To achieve this, it is crucial for the company to consider fresh perspectives and ideas, ensuring it remains at the cutting edge of aerospace technology and business strategy.

In aerospace industry, the market doesn’t always know what users want. It’s definitely not flying cars or just larger in size.
