# Hello World!

*Substack · Domi’s Mind Waves · Manifesto · By Domi Jin (November 2024)*

## I’m starting my Substack channel!

I’ve occasionally posted my thoughts in different places: [personal website](https://domij.info/), [GitHub](https://github.com/domijin/LibertyEarth), [Medium](https://medium.com/@domijin), X, WeChat, etc. But I want to be more **consistent and transparent to share my self-awareness, reflections, and path to self-realization & actualization**.

Here are some reasons why I am transitioning to Substack:

1. Medium has a paywall, whereas Substack is more flexible: I’m making all my posts free to subscribe.
2. Ideas are cheap; execution and the learnings from execution are more valuable, and ideas born from those learnings are more insightful.
3. Why this, why now: I’m feeling more comfortable with being uncomfortable, as I’ve gone through enough struggle and anxiety about my struggle that I hope I’ve outgrown the anxiety.

I appreciate your attention, so I would like you to know that my writings will combine raw notes with edited and revised detailed posts with the help of LLMs. (I am doing this only to improve my writing skills, so I can rely less on LLMs while delivering equally effective and more concise points in an English-reader-friendly way.)

Here is a quote I really like in Chinese (it is often credited to Steve Jobs, though I have never found the original):

> “**我们都在穷尽自己仅有的一点天赋，来表达内心深处的感受！**”
>
> Translated back into English: “We exhaust our gifts for our whole lives, to express the feelings deep in our soul.”

I see myself as someone “**Driven by intellectual honesty, empathy, and a passion for making a lasting impact for the greater good.**” I noted on [my personal website](https://domij.info/) back in 2018 that my vision centers on **empowering individuals and organizations to tackle society’s most pressing challenges collaboratively, as I believe humanity’s greatest achievements arise from collective efforts**.

I’m now pursuing the second phase of life: helping make the world a little bit different through social change and, hopefully, making a difference.

There are multiple pathways to reach that: creative writing (picture a better world), investment (working/donating/investing to make a better world), entrepreneurship (dedicate to realize a better world), education (empower people to make a better world), etc. There is no bias or limit in doing all these together, but I do think creative writing is the least distraction to the other three options.

More self-reflection and sharing will be coming. Stay tuned!

Hope my writing has prompted you to spark some thinking. P.S. If I can write, you can write!
