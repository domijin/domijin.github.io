- **March 31, 2023:** [Boeing’s Future at Stake: The Growing Challenge from SpaceX](https://domijin.medium.com/boeings-future-at-stake-the-growing-challenge-from-spacex-177b63b10882) — this is my Medium post commenting about Boeing and SpaceX’s competition in aerospace, demonstrating my business sense with first-principles thinking as well as my exposure in this space driven by curiosity & unique background.
- **October 9, 2023:** [Why is some professional handwriting illegible?](https://medium.com/@domijin/why-is-some-professional-handwriting-illegible-d07bd8acb818) — this is my Medium post commenting about doctors’ illegible handwriting — trying to unfold the economic, psychological & societal reasons behind this man-made information barrier (like a paywall, which motivated me to write on Substack instead).
- **October 10, 2023:** [Observation: the dilemma and cultural difference behind the eVTOL field](https://domijin.medium.com/observation-the-dilemma-and-cultural-difference-behind-the-evtol-field-df42e91af109) — this is my Medium post commenting about the dilemma and cultural difference in regulation and commercial attempts to capture the “first-mover advantage” in the eVTOL field — unveiling the potential envy beyond the denial, anger, and ignorance reaction.
- **October 10, 2023:** [From Traffic Lights to Life’s Direction](https://domijin.medium.com/from-traffic-lights-to-lifes-direction-57176d5d7a06) — this is my Medium post reflecting on my life from a traffic light countdown feature offered in a Chinese navigation app — I am really proud of my cross-cultural background and the inspirations I can resonate with from different perspectives.
- **October 9, 2024:** [Rethinking Digital Advertising: From Guesswork to User Engagement](https://domijin.medium.com/rethinking-digital-advertising-from-guesswork-to-user-engagement-6e1916a2aced) & [The Future of Advertising — Moving Beyond Relevance to Demand Profiling and Dynamic Personalization](https://domijin.medium.com/the-future-of-advertising-moving-beyond-relevance-to-demand-profiling-and-dynamic-personalization-5f4ccc03697c) — these are my Medium posts sharing my insight about the future of advertising — this is the beginning of a series of reflections about the attention/surveillance economy, demand-driven economy, spreading social dynamics & mental health evolution, which recalibrated the path & approach to fulfill a mission to liberate people from the trend of the “inception” economy.
- **November 21, 2024:** [Trace of AppLovin’s 7x Annual Hypergrowth](https://medium.com/@domijin/trace-of-applovins-7x-annual-hypergrowth-39dcb154f5d0) — this is my Medium post unfolding the challenges and opportunities in this more-profitable-than-ever advertising business — leveraging my insights from working in this fast-moving, “aggressive” domain to filter audiences who might see my talents & vision to reach out, support, or collaborate toward a better future.
- **November 25, 2024:** [Why TikTok is so popular](https://domijin.medium.com/why-tiktok-is-so-popular-6fcc7b678bc7) — repeater-like post, nothing super unique.
- **November 25, 2024:** [Is Dark Mode Doing More Harm Than Good? A Deeper Look at Our Choices](https://medium.com/@domijin/is-dark-mode-doing-more-harm-than-good-a-deeper-look-at-our-choices-a9685daad170) — this is my Medium post commenting about the side risks of Dark Mode and my concerns about the agile workflow in the tech industry reflecting a cultural preference of dismissing social responsibility (externalities) for career and economic capital gain.
- **November 26, 2024:** [Another team building Skynet, in the name of Jarvis — AMA](https://medium.com/@domijin/another-team-building-skynet-in-the-name-of-jarvis-ama-163389dc5285) — this is my Medium post sharing my involvement and understanding of the project “ThirdEar/TwinMind” that I co-founded, before stepping down as advisor to avoid conflicts of interest and for the rescue of our friendship.
