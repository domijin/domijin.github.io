### Daily updates

- Standup
  - Completed task
  - Tasks with blockers
  - Next thing to do
- Self-discipline
  - Project board update
  - Monday task-board update
  - CRM update → talked-to users

### Weekly updates

- Team discuss: review, reflect, planning, collaborate
- YC Startup School Weekly Update
  - Primary metrics
    - Weeks to launch
    - Talked Users
  - Goals & Morale
- Substack: weekly/bi-weekly update with 1–2 stories

### How I collaborate

- Share calendar for inclusion: show what I’m doing, when I’m available, what I will be working on (add tasks on calendar to show my focus — need to move from Apple Reminder to Google Reminder)
- Fractional & asynchronous contribution: others can check their availability and decide if they want to know the problem, progress, and share their experience

A few personal principles and promises:

- Rest & balance is important!
- Family is important!
- No need to feel obligated. If others are not available, it’s fine. Try to do it yourself & ask for feedback.
- This might not seem to be efficient, but this often happens when we are actually innovating.
