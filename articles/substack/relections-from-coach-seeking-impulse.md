# Reflections from coach-seeking impulse

*Chapter III · Human Agency, Older People’s Independence and Lifelong Play — By Domi Jin (December 2024)*

Here are the lessons I learned.

- 

Coaching is just guided self reflection, it will only work when someone is intellectually honest and willing to step out of comfort zone to build up the context.
- 

The effectiveness depends on how relevant the coach and you are from the context built up. So authenticity and communication efficiency matters.
- 

It is a constantly evolving process, honesty and willingness to grow should be mutuall.
- 

Be cautious about transference. Your intimate partner is the best coach and coach candidate.
- 

There is only a thin line between coach and mentor: is the person willing to go beyond reflection, and allow the specific cases where the roles are reversed, so to be in a mutually empowering relationship.
- 

There is always a limit that either coaching becomes less important than practicing, or growth is just ahead of the rate of the practical world bounded by physics laws. The latter case is extremely rare across the history, and those decisive minds are the glory of our species.
- 

Coaching and mentoring can happen from anywhere with any person, it only depends on the openness and willingness of the mind, and it is a trusted-building relationship.

Words (lessons) are cheap, actions (practices) matter.
