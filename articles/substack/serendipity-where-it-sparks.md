# Serendipity: where it sparks

*Chapter IV · Institutions, Incentives and Possible Futures — By Domi Jin (December 2024)*

## **A Childhood Rooted in Stories and Tradition**

I have always been drawn to stories. Growing up, I found myself gravitating toward conversations with seniors. Perhaps it stemmed from my unique upbringing. My parents, often busy working, left me in the company of older relatives or neighbors.  With my parents busy working, and my grandparents passing early, I often spent time with older relatives and neighbors. Their stories of challenges and triumphs became a rich source of wisdom, opening my eyes to the breadth of human experience.

My affinity for the elderly and children did not go unnoticed. Friends often remarked on how naturally I connected with them, calling it a gift of “亲和力”—an innate ability to make people feel at ease. From chatting with teachers and neighbors to forming friendships with strangers at dining table, traveling, community events or family gathering, I’ve always found joy in building relationships. This connection extends to my involvement in activities that engage seniors, such as volunteering at garden & libraries, attending lawn bowling events, and participating in living history and Renaissance fairs. These moments have shaped my understanding of intergenerational bonds and the value of diverse perspectives.

## **Living with Curiosity and Gratitude**

My life has been guided by open-ended curiosity and a deep appreciation for serendipity—those unplanned moments that spark growth and insight. Influenced by Confucian values of “礼” (ritual propriety) and “仁” (benevolence), I’ve cultivated respect, humility, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

Recognizing the fleeting nature of life has further inspired me (studying astrophysics in universe timescale also contributes to this). As the reflection "人就这么一辈子" reminds us, life is brief, and each moment is irreversible. The words of Su Dongpo, "寄蜉蝣于天地，渺沧海之一粟" resonate deeply, urging me to cherish each instant and channel my limited time into meaningful action. I'm known by my friends for my diverse interests, which you may find the trace from our conversation or some of my past [footprints](https://domijin.github.io/pages/footprint.html).

## **Standing on the Shoulders of Giants**

The phrase “standing on the shoulders of giants” has shaped my intellectual journey. Yet, I’ve come to realize that standing isn’t enough. While giants illuminate paths, more innovations lie in finding what they might have missed. It requires courage to question, explore, and build beyond existing paradigms.

My approach has always been interdisciplinary. I’ve sought to understand the different peaks those decisive giants have climbed and aimed to connect these peaks (dots). By connecting insights from astrophysics, machine learning, and human-computer interaction, I aim to build a “highland” of understanding—a vantage point from which to see further and think deeper. Each summit climbed reveals new horizons, and this broader perspective enables me to envision solutions that transcend individual disciplines.

## **Collaboration Through Authenticity**

I've realized that collaboration is essential. Authenticity, grounded in mutual respect and understanding, is important to foster trust and align values across diverse groups. Following this principle, a diverse team of like-minded individuals, each bringing complementary strengths to the table is coming to shape. In traditional Chinese terms, we are “八仙过海，各显神通”—a collective united by shared goals yet enriched by individual talents.

I’m deeply grateful for the trust and support I've received from this family and friends. Their belief in me fuels my determination and inspires our shared commitment to meaningful progress.

## **The Mission Ahead**

As I look forward, I’m filled with conviction. This team is ready to learn, act, and share together. We will identify our shared strengths and gaps, and build consensus rooted in mutual trust, advancing toward the narrow gate of ultimate fulfillment—a sense of purpose and actualization. While the journey may never be fully accomplished, like Sisyphus pushing his boulder, the striving itself brings meaning and inspires others to join and contribute.

## **Final Thoughts**

Serendipity has been the invisible thread weaving my life together. From childhood conversations with seniors to the timely & relevant connections that contributes to the journey. These moments have inspired me to "Carpe diem. Seize the day, boys. Make your lives extraordinary."--Dead Poets Society.
