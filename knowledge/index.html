02 · How to beat ChatGPT

The proposal in my 2023 sketch: combine correlated memories with observations from the world and explicit symbolic operations. A hypothesis to test, not a benchmark result.

Start with the related concepts

For: What does a gravitational-wave standard siren let us measure?

  1. Use the highlighted concepts to frame a candidate explanation. Relevance alone does not establish that it is true.
  2. Identify the source and assumptions behind the candidate explanation.
  3. Check the reasoning against the source; mark any claim that still needs a calculation or test.
  4. Compare the result with an independent check. Feed confirmed findings back into the knowledge graph; keep unresolved claims open.

These switches illustrate a workflow. They do not fetch evidence or execute a calculation.
