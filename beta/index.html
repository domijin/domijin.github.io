Why messaging

Most AI tools assume you are sitting in front of a browser with full attention.

This beta explores AI as a lightweight communication layer — accessible through messaging, useful for quick retrieval and personal support, and shaped by what people actually ask for.

Beta expectations

Early, manual, and intentionally small.

Access is invite-only for now. Every signup is reviewed manually before activation.

  • Manual approval is required before activation.
  • Approval and onboarding may take up to 12 hours.
  • You will receive a confirmation message if approved.
  • Early limitations, rough edges, and occasional misses are expected.
  • Feedback is part of the beta and helps decide what gets built next.

Limitations and safety

Experimental software, not a replacement for professional or emergency help.

This service is not a replacement for 911, emergency services, medical professionals, legal professionals, financial advisors, or human caregivers. In emergencies, contact local emergency services directly.
