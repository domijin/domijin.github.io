01 / Research

Computational astrophysics

Ph.D. Physics (2013–2018) · LSST Data Science Fellow

I began in computational astrophysics, studying stellar dynamics and binary black holes through numerical simulation and statistical modelling.

  • ▹3,240 MOCCA globular cluster simulations (>100 TB, 25k node-hours on TACC) modelling 662,772 clusters within 30 Mpc.
  • ▹Extracted 17.88M inspiralling binary black holes and 86,939 mergers; derived a lower-bound merger rate of 587 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹ and 17 LISA standard sirens.
  • ▹LSST Data Science Fellowship: statistical modelling, image processing and reproducible research workflows.

How I work

Working together

Good work depends on more than individual ability. Shared context, trust, complementary skills and sensible incentives matter, as does room for people to exercise their own judgement.

Usefulness

I judge technology by what it helps people do: understand a problem, make a choice, learn a skill or spend their time better. Technical elegance is valuable when it serves that purpose.

Testing the idea

I try to make assumptions explicit, build something that can be tested and pay attention to the result. Revising an explanation is part of the work.
